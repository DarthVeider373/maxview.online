About Chopan Kebab

Authentic Afghan cuisine crafted with passion, tradition, and the finest halal ingredients.

Bringing Afghan Flavours Since 2017

Chopan Kebab was founded in Edgware with a passion for sharing authentic Afghan cuisine. Our name 'Chopan' means shepherd — representing the simplicity and richness of traditional Afghan cooking.

Every dish is prepared fresh using traditional recipes passed down through generations. We use only the finest halal meats, fresh vegetables, and authentic Afghan spices to create flavours that transport you straight to Kabul.

Whether you're dining in, ordering takeaway, or booking our catering for your special event — we promise an unforgettable experience of Afghan hospitality and flavour.

Our Menu Highlights

A taste of our most popular dishes — all freshly prepared with traditional Afghan recipes and 100% halal ingredients.

🥙

Lamb Chopan Kebab

Tender marinated lamb chops grilled over charcoal to perfection. Served with fresh naan and chutneys.

🍚

Kabuli Pulao

Afghanistan's national dish — fragrant rice with tender lamb, caramelised carrots and sweet raisins. A must-try.

🍖

Chapli Kebab

Spiced minced meat patties with tomatoes, coriander and green chillies. Crispy on the outside, juicy inside.

🫓

Fresh Afghan Naan

Baked fresh to order in our traditional oven. Soft, fluffy and perfect for scooping up our signature dishes.

🥗

Bolani & Mantu

Traditional Afghan starters — stuffed flatbread with leeks/potato and steamed dumplings with spiced lamb and yoghurt sauce.

🍨

Firni & Sheer Yakh

Traditional Afghan desserts — creamy milk pudding with cardamom and pistachios, plus our homemade Afghan ice cream.
